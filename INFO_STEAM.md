[h1]OPERATION ROYAL ALLIANCE (ORA)[/h1]
[h2]RACS SPECIAL OPERATIONS COMMAND[/h2]

[h3]SITREP[/h3]
Insurgent forces have seized control of strategic sectors. Armed cells operate within the civilian population while several critical infrastructures are threatened.

The [b]Royal Army Corps of Sahrani (RACS)[/b] deploys a rapid response unit to restore security, gather intelligence, and neutralize emerging threats.

[i]You are the vanguard of this operation.[/i]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]MISSION[/h2]
Conduct special operations behind enemy lines, secure civilian populations, and prevent armed groups from further destabilizing the region.

Each deployment is dynamically generated with randomized operational schedules (time of day) and offers varied objectives, guaranteeing near-limitless replayability.

[b]You will be deployed on site. Request and manage your mission tasks directly from the action menu (scroll wheel).[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[/list]
